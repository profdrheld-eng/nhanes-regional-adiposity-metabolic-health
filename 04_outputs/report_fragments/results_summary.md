# Results draft

## Sample

The harmonized source contained 39,156 records. The primary complete-case population included 3,971 participants, including 1,963 women and 2,008 men. Metabolic dysfunction was present in 1,643 unweighted participants.

## Primary association

Per one pooled standard deviation higher log leg-to-trunk fat ratio, the adjusted association was PR 0.71, 95% CI 0.66 to 0.77, p=1.8e-11 in men and PR 0.64, 95% CI 0.60 to 0.69, p=1.58e-15 in women. The direct exposure-by-sex interaction was PR 0.90, 95% CI 0.83 to 0.98, p=0.0137. These estimates are cross-sectional associations and do not identify lipedema or establish protection.

## Temporal prediction

The best temporal-test model by weighted log loss was penalized logistic using the D_dxa panel (ROC AUC 0.811, 95% cluster-bootstrap CI 0.760 to 0.868; Brier score 0.170; calibration slope 0.956). Adding DXA variables to BMI and waist increased ROC AUC by +0.033 (95% paired cluster-bootstrap CI 0.011 to 0.055) for penalized logistic regression and by +0.031 for XGBoost.
