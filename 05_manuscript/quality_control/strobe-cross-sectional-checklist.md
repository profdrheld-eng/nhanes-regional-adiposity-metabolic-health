# STROBE cross-sectional reporting checklist

Current companion-file structure, 5 October 2026. All applicable cross-sectional items are mapped below. Locations refer to section headings and labelled objects, which remain stable when pages reflow. M = Manuscript; S = Supplementary File. This is an item-by-item reporting assessment, not certification of study quality or complete compliance. Continuous exposure is retained; descriptive characteristics are stratified by sex rather than an arbitrary dichotomy of exposure.

Official checklist: https://www.strobe-statement.org/fileadmin/Strobe/uploads/checklists/STROBE_checklist_v4_cross-sectional.pdf
